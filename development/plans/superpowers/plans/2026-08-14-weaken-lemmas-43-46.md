## Approved Design

Immediately before Lemma 43, introduce local notation for a prime divisor \((Z,\xi)\):

\[
K_\xi=\operatorname{Frac}(\widehat{\mathcal O}_{X,\xi}),
\]

and write \(I_{K_\xi}\) and \(P_{K_\xi}\) for its inertia and wild inertia subgroups. For a finite constant abelian group \(G\), a restricted \(G\)-torsor has a character \(\rho:G_{K_\xi}\to G\). It is unramified exactly when \(\rho(I_{K_\xi})=1\), and tame exactly when \(\rho(P_{K_\xi})=1\).

The four lemmas then have these roles:

1. Lemma 43: tame ramification is preserved under contracted products of torsors with the same structure group.
2. Lemma 44: tame ramification and unramifiedness are invariant under the weakly unramified, separable-residue base changes used in the thesis.
3. Lemma 45: tame ramification is preserved under fibre products of torsors with different structure groups.
4. Lemma 46: induction along an injective group homomorphism preserves the kernel of the local character; local numerical bounds, tameness, and unramifiedness follow as specializations.

The only downstream proposition requiring a mathematical weakening is Proposition 64: replace its numerical bound by a tame-ramification hypothesis and conclusion. Its sole consumer, Lemma 65, already supplies and needs only tameness.

- [ ] **Step 2: Replace Lemma 43 and its proof**

Use this statement and proof, preserving the existing label:

```latex
\begin{lemma}\label{lemma:bounding_ramification_of_contracted_product}
Let $G$ be a finite abelian group, let $X$ be a locally Noetherian scheme,
let $U\subset X$ be a dense open, and let $(Z,\xi)$ be a prime divisor in
$X\setminus U$. If $\cP_1$ and $\cP_2$ are $G$-torsors on
$U_{\mathrm{\acute et}}$ that are tamely ramified at $(Z,\xi)$, then
$\cP_1\otimes^G\cP_2$ is tamely ramified at $(Z,\xi)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $K=K_\xi$, and let $\rho_1,\rho_2:G_K\to G$ be the characters of the
two restricted torsors. Tameness gives
$\rho_1(P_K)=\rho_2(P_K)=\{1\}$. The character of the contracted product is
$\rho_1+\rho_2$, so it also kills $P_K$.
\end{proof}
```

```latex
\begin{lemma}\label{lemma:descend_ramification_along_etale}
Let $f:X\to Y$ be a morphism of locally Noetherian schemes. Let
$U_X\subset X$ and $U_Y\subset Y$ be dense open subschemes such that
$f^{-1}(U_Y)\subset U_X$. Let $(Z_X,\xi_X)$ and $(Z_Y,\xi_Y)$ be prime
divisors in the respective complements, and suppose $f(\xi_X)=\xi_Y$.
Assume that
\[
\Oo_{Y,\xi_Y}\longrightarrow\Oo_{X,\xi_X}
\]
is weakly unramified and has separable residue-field extension. If $\cP$ is
a $G$-torsor over $U_Y$, then $f^{-1}\cP$ is tamely ramified at $\xi_X$ if
and only if $\cP$ is tamely ramified at $\xi_Y$. The analogous equivalence
holds for unramifiedness.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Pass to the completed local fields at $\xi_Y$ and $\xi_X$. Tame
ramification and unramifiedness are invariant under a weakly unramified
extension of complete discretely valued fields with separable residue-field
extension. Applying this to the restricted torsor proves both equivalences.
\end{proof}
```

- [ ] **Step 4: Replace Lemma 45 and its proof**

```latex
\begin{lemma}\label{lemma:ramification_of_fiber_product}
Let $G_1,G_2$ be finite abelian groups, let $X$ be a locally Noetherian
scheme, let $U\subset X$ be a dense open, and let $(Z,\xi)$ be a prime
divisor in $X\setminus U$. Let $\cP_i$ be a $G_i$-torsor on
$U_{\mathrm{\acute et}}$ for $i=1,2$. If both $\cP_1$ and $\cP_2$ are
tamely ramified at $(Z,\xi)$, then the $(G_1\times G_2)$-torsor
$\cP_1\times_U\cP_2$ is tamely ramified at $(Z,\xi)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $K=K_\xi$, and let $\rho_i:G_K\to G_i$ be the character of
$\cP_i|_{\Spec(K)}$. The character of the fibre product is
$(\rho_1,\rho_2)$. Since each $\rho_i$ kills $P_K$, their product character
kills $P_K$ as well.
\end{proof}
```

- [ ] **Step 5: Replace Lemma 46 by a local kernel lemma**

Use a complete local field in the statement, so numerical bounds are mentioned only where they have already been defined:

```latex
\begin{lemma}\label{lemma:ramification_of_induced_torsor}
Let $K$ be a complete discretely valued field, let
$\iota:H\hookrightarrow G$ be an injective homomorphism of finite abelian
groups, and let $\cQ$ be an $H$-torsor over $\Spec(K)$ with character
$\chi:G_K\to H$. For every closed subgroup $N\subset G_K$, the character
of the induced $G$-torsor $\iota_*\cQ$ satisfies
\[
    (\iota\circ\chi)(N)=\{1\}
    \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
    \chi(N)=\{1\}.
\]
Consequently, induction preserves every locally defined upper-numbering
ramification bound, tame ramification, and unramifiedness.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The character of $\iota_*\cQ$ is $\iota\circ\chi$. Since $\iota$ is
injective,
\[
    \ker(\iota\circ\chi)=\ker(\chi),
\]
which gives the asserted equivalence for every $N$. Apply it with
$N=G_K^r$, $N=P_K$, and $N=I_K$, respectively.
\end{proof}
```
